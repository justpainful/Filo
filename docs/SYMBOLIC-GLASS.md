# Symbolic Glass and Symbolic Style icons (0.1.8)

Two new defaults. Settings > Appearance keeps every earlier material and icon pack as an alternative:
Liquid Glass, Soft Glass, Acrylic and Glassy UI; Classic, Pebble, Circuit and Facet. Settings files written before 0.1.8 move to the new defaults once;
a choice made afterwards is kept.

## Symbolic Glass (default material)

The glass material of the owner's **Symbolic** app, ported from its measured presets (`src/glass/presets/glass.presets.v0.3.json`, read only). Filo windows are dialogs, so
the `menu` material (menus, pop-ups and dialogs) is used. Filo is native and Symbolic is a WebGL engine, so this is a port of the parameters and the edge model, not a copy of code.

| Layer | Light | Dark | Preset value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live backdrop blur | 9 DIP | 9 DIP | `menu.blurSigmaDip` |
| Veil over the glass | white 50 % | 2 % | `tintAlphaLight` / `tintAlphaDark` |
| Backdrop passed through | 1 | x 0.38 (hue kept) | `darkTransmission` |
| Rim light | peak .8, width 1.16 DIP, inset .6 DIP, axial along 214 deg (bright top-left and bottom-right, dim across) | same x 0.45 | `rimWhitePeakAlpha`, `rimWidthDip`, `rimAxisDeg`, `darkRimGain` |
| Outline | .65 DIP, black 16.5 % | white 12 % | `borderAlphaLight/Dark` |
| Shadows | near .11 / sigma 3.1 / down 9.2, far .03 | near .15, far .06 | `nearShadow`, `farShadow` |
| Text | #1D1D1F / #6E6E73 / #8E8E93 | #F5F5F7 / #AEAEB2 / #8E8E93 | Symbolic `tokens.css` |

Implementation: `src/gfx/live_composition.cpp` (blur 9 and the dark dimming, with the same edge-refraction layers as Liquid Glass), `src/ui/painter.cpp`
(`SymbolicShadow`, `SymbolicBody`, `SymbolicRim`), `src/ui/dialog_window.cpp` (ink, shadow, body and rim selection), `src/ui/settings_window.cpp` (the choice and its **Edge refraction** slider).

Not ported: the adaptive light/dark regime of the engine (Filo keeps its explicit theme), the physical lens (Filo's refraction layers are used), the hover/press light, glass over glass.
The 214 degree edge light is drawn with a gradient stroke, so it is an approximation of the shader. Hidden renders (`--tool-shot ... --symbolic`, `--download-shot`) were looked at in light and dark;
the live compositor over a moving desktop was **not** watched.

## Symbolic Style icons (default icon pack)

An **original** pack drawn for Filo (`assets/icon-packs/00-symbolic.json`, generated by `tools/generate_icon_packs.py`): rounded line icons of one even weight (1.75) with a soft second tone
where Pebble has a silhouette. All 68 icons are covered, so no icon falls back to another pack.

**SF Symbols are not bundled.** The Symbolic app's library holds SF Symbols outlines derived from Apple's SF Pro font files. Apple's licence limits SF Symbols to work for Apple platforms, and the owner
cannot grant a Windows installer the right to carry Apple's artwork, so those outlines were deliberately not copied into Filo. The pack imitates the *style* (even rounded line, quiet second tone)
with Filo's own drawings. If the owner wants real SF Symbols for personal use, they can be added as a per-user pack in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Filo\icon-packs` (Filo loads those; format in ICON-PACKS-2026-10-21.md);
that stays on that PC and is not part of the installer.
